An entry for the make.fun competition `u-eager-stork-81-9647-the-houseplant-out-of-office-7c586047` — "The Houseplant Out-of-Office".

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network) writes one line to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`): the funniest
out-of-office auto-reply a houseplant would send.

The reply is 111 characters (Unicode code points), within the competition's
1–120 character limit:

> Out of office. My human left for two weeks and my only water is a neighbour who waves at me through the window.

The joke: the plant's survival depends on its one water source — a neighbour
who only waves at it through the glass instead of coming in to water it.
